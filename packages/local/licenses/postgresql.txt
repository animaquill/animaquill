==============================================================================
PostgreSQL, as it comes with `npx animaquill`
==============================================================================
The backend of `npx animaquill` starts PostgreSQL from the package
@embedded-postgres/<platform> (https://github.com/leinelissen/embedded-postgres,
its own code under the MIT License), which npm installs next to this package
for the platform it runs on. AnimaQuill runs those programs as they are, as
programs of their own; it changes nothing of them. They and the libraries that
come with them are under their own licenses:

- PostgreSQL (initdb, pg_ctl, postgres and its libraries): the PostgreSQL
  License, below. Source: https://www.postgresql.org/ftp/source/
- OpenSSL (libssl, libcrypto): OpenSSL 3 under the Apache License 2.0; the
  1.1 series of the Linux packages under the OpenSSL License and the original
  SSLeay License. https://www.openssl.org/source/license.html
- ICU (libicu*): the Unicode License (ICU License).
  https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/main/LICENSE
- zlib: the zlib License. https://zlib.net/zlib_license.html
- libxml2 and libxslt: the MIT License. https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/libxml2
  and https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/libxslt
- liblzma (XZ Utils): public domain / 0BSD. https://tukaani.org/xz/
- zstd: the BSD License. https://github.com/facebook/zstd
- lz4: the BSD 2-Clause License. https://github.com/lz4/lz4
- OSSP uuid / libuuid: MIT-style licenses. http://www.ossp.org/pkg/lib/uuid/
- Windows only, libcurl: the curl License. https://curl.se/docs/copyright.html
- Windows only, loaded by PostgreSQL as it is: GNU gettext's runtime
  (libintl-9.dll) and GNU libiconv (libiconv-2.dll), under the GNU Lesser
  General Public License 2.1. They are linked dynamically, as they come, and
  can be replaced by other builds of the same libraries. Their sources:
  https://www.gnu.org/software/gettext/ (https://ftp.gnu.org/gnu/gettext/)
  and https://www.gnu.org/software/libiconv/ (https://ftp.gnu.org/gnu/libiconv/).
  The text of the LGPL 2.1: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.html
- Windows only, unused by AnimaQuill: the wxWidgets libraries (wx*.dll) in the
  same package, under the wxWindows Library Licence. https://www.wxwidgets.org/about/licence/
